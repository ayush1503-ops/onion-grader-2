How It Works

From photo to grade, in three steps.

The whole pipeline runs on one photo — no special hardware, no manual measurements.

01

Upload Image

Take one overhead photo of your onions in a single layer, ideally with a ₹10 coin in the frame for a true millimetre scale.

02

Computer Vision Analysis

The detector finds every onion, separates touching ones, measures each diameter, and reads surface colours for damage, rot and sprouting.

03

Get Quality Grade

You receive an inspection report: grade A / URS / Reject per onion, size distribution, estimated weight and downloadable files.

Applications

Built for everyone who handles onions.

Farmers Check the grade before taking the harvest to the mandi — know what a fair price looks like.
Quality Control Verify supplier lots against size and defect specifications in seconds, with a photo record.
Storage & Sorting Sort onions into size windows before cold storage and catch sprouting or rotting bulbs early.
Food Processing Standardise input lots by diameter so slicing and dicing lines run uniformly.
Agricultural Research Measure size distributions across varieties and trials from simple field photos.
About

An honest onion grader, not a magic box.

Onion Analyser was built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (problem statement SIH26031). It combines classical computer vision — contour detection, watershed separation, colour analysis and coin-based scale calibration — with a trained random-forest surface classifier and an optional YOLOv8 detector.

Everything the app reports is measured from your photo: onion count, diameter in millimetres, visible surface condition and an estimated weight from a calibratable mass model. Accuracy claims are only made when measured on a real labelled test set, and the measured numbers are published openly.

The app also runs fully offline as an installable page for fields with poor connectivity.

What a photo can & cannot show

  • Count every visible onion, even in a pile (layer by layer)
  • Measure diameter in true millimetres when a coin is in frame
  • Spot visible surface defects: dark rot patches, bruising, sprouts
  • Estimate total weight from the measured size distribution
  • Internal rot, internal damage or moisture — invisible to any camera
  • Onions completely hidden under other onions
  • Disease inside the bulb without surface symptoms
