🪙 Nothing to set — it's automatic. If a coin is visible in the photo it is detected by itself (assumed ₹10/₹2 = 27 mm). No coin? Sizes are honest estimates (you'll see a ⚠️ warning).

📸 Photo tips

  • Onions in one layer on a light background (white cloth / paper).
  • Optional but best: put a coin flat in the photo (₹10/₹2 = exact mm). No coin works too — sizes become estimates.
  • Hold the phone directly above, parallel to the table.
  • Good, even light. No harsh shadows.
Two AI engines:
◉ Classic CV — threshold + watershed counting. Works instantly, no training.
⚡ YOLOv8 — neural detection. Needs your fine-tuned models/onion_yolo.pt (train with train_yolo.py). Toggle it in the top bar.
HONEST CAPABILITIES

What this AI can and cannot see.

✓ It CAN (from the photo)

  • ●Count every onion — touching ones split with watershed
  • ●Measure each diameter in mm (coin as ruler)
  • ●See VISIBLE surface defects: green sprouts, dark rot patches, brown damage
  • ●Compute % Grade A / URS / Reject + a digital report

✗ It CANNOT (never from a normal photo)

  • ●Internal rot hidden under healthy-looking skin
  • ●Internal damage or moisture content
  • ●Smell, firmness, weight
  • ●“Guaranteed accuracy” — thresholds are demo starting points; real accuracy must be measured on a labeled test set
HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a transparent grade.

01

Capture

Live camera or a photo. A coin sits next to the batch — it is the exact millimetre ruler for every measurement.

02

Analyze

Classic CV (Otsu → watershed → HSV color rules) or a fine-tuned YOLOv8 neural detector. Same honest grading logic on top.

03

Report

Annotated image, JSON + text reports and a report card — with batch ID, date/time and the honesty disclaimer. Zero subjectivity.
